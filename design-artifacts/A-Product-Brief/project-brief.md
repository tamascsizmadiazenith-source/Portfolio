# Project Brief: Tamas Csizmadia - Fineart Photos and mountain spirit

## Project Overview
**Vision & Mission**: To create a premium, high-end digital presence for photographer Tamas Csizmadia, showcasing his fine art photography focused on landscapes, nightscapes, and the "mountain spirit." The platform should serve as both an immersive gallery experience and a professional storefront that facilitates the sale of high-quality prints.

**Mission**: To bridge the gap between breathtaking visual storytelling and a seamless e-commerce experience, allowing collectors and curators to discover, appreciate, and acquire unique photographic works with ease and confidence.

## Target Audience
1.  **Print Buyers (Collectors/Auction Attendees)**: Individuals looking for high-quality, atmospheric art for their homes or collections. They value aesthetics, print quality options, and a smooth purchasing process.
2.  **Exhibition Curators & Gallery Owners**: Professionals seeking to discover new talent and high-caliber work for exhibitions. They require a professional, polished presentation that demonstrates the artist's capability and scale of work.

## Core Features & Functionality
*   **Immersive Photo Gallery**: A categorized gallery (by theme/location) featuring 100-200 high-resolution images.
*   **Virtual Wall Preview**: An interactive feature allowing users to see how a photo would look on a "virtual wall" at various sizes, helping them visualize the impact in their own space.
*   **Shopify Integration (E-commerce)**: A robust ordering system integrated with Shopify to handle:
    *   Selection of medium (e.g., Paper vs. Canvas).
    *   Various size options.
    *   Integrated checkout and payment processing.
*   **Image Protection**: Implementation of advanced measures (e.g., watermarking, disabling right-click/save, low-res previews) to prevent unauthorized downloads while maintaining a high-quality viewing experience.
*   **Contact & Inquiry System**: (See Contact Flow below).

## User Experience & Aesthetic
*   **Aesthetic**: Dark background, atmospheric, and modern. The design should feel "premium" and "high-end," allowing the colors and light of the photography to stand's out.
*   **Brand Voice**: Professional, adventurous, and evocative. It should evoke the feeling of chasing light and exploring hidden mountain places.
*   **Animations & Motion**: 
    *   Slow zoom animation on the headline cover to create an immediate sense of depth and immersion.
    *   Smooth scrolling animations throughout the site to enhance the premium feel.

## Content Structure
*   **Main Page**: Headline cover with immersive animation, followed by featured highlights.
*   **Gallery**: Categorized by themes (e.g., Nightscapes, Alpine Peaks) or locations.
*   **About Page**: A professional biography and artist statement focusing on the "mountain spirit" and the journey of capturing light.

## Contact Flow Proposal
To maintain professionalism while facilitating high-value inquiries, I propose a dual-layered approach:
1.  **Integrated Inquiry Buttons**: Each photo in the gallery will feature a subtle, elegant "Inquire about this piece" button. This allows curators or collectors to express interest directly from the context of the artwork.
2.  **Dedicated Professional Contact Page**: A clean, well-structured contact page for broader inquiries (exhibitions, commissions, press). This page will include a professional form and direct contact details, ensuring that high-level professional communication is streamlined.

## Success Criteria
*   **Seamless E-commerce**: Successful integration of Shopify with zero friction in the selection and checkout process.
*   **Visual Impact**: The website's aesthetic successfully reflects the "premium" and "atmospheric" nature of the photography.
*   **User Engagement**: High retention rates on the gallery pages and successful use of the "Virtual Wall" feature.
*   **Security**: Effective implementation of image protection without compromising the user experience.
